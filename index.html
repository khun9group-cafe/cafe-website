Our story

A taste of Thailand,
a table in Palmerston

Inspired by Thai life, craft, and the canals of the floating market, KHUN9 is a modern reading of heritage — breakfast through lunch, seven days.

Stilt houses on the mural, rattan and brass on the wall, boat noodles and Sum Tum on the pass. From our kitchen at Oasis Shopping Village to your table.
